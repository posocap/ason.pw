About

A selfish library

This library exists for one reason: I want it. I built it to organize my own thinking, keep my own notes where I can find them, and give my own writing a home. Nobody asked me to, and nobody is paying me to.

I take no donations, no sponsorships and no ads. Every hour and every dollar is mine, and so is every decision about what goes on the shelves. I do not owe the library an audience, and the audience does not owe it support.

That is the whole arrangement, and I think it is the honest one: I pursue my own interest, in the open, and anyone who finds value in the result is welcome to it. Trade, not charity, in both directions.

What you will find

  • Remixed notes, always crediting the original source.
  • Original essays and thought experiments.
  • Links to free public material by others.
