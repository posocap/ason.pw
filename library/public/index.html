The Ason Peikoff Library

A personal library of philosophy, study notes and original writing. Built by one person, for the benefit of that person — and open to anyone who finds it useful.

Explore the Library

Who is Ason Peikoff?

A writer and student of philosophy who publishes Postsocialist Press and keeps this library for his own use: notes, remixes, essays and the occasional thought experiment.

What is this library?

A curated shelf: pointers to excellent public material, my own condensed notes and remixes of it, and original essays. Every item says where it came from and what I added.

Who is this site for?

  • Me, first. That is the point.
  • Students of philosophy and anyone curious about Objectivism.
  • Anyone building their own course of study.
